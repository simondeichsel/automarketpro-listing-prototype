AutoMarketPro Dealer App: weekly car check

Creating a listing is already automated (VIN decoding, AI text, DMS feeds). This prototype covers what the data can't know, after publishing, for every listing channel.

  1. Weekly car check: at most 3 cars, sorted by price × days online, each with the one gap that matters
  2. What the rating can't see: warranty, condition, extras added in one tap, comparison radius set by the dealer, rating explained with the comparison cars
  3. Answer once: questions buyers keep asking, answered in the listing
  4. Missing photos: guided shots for exactly what's missing

Principles: no price-cut recommendations, the dealer's price is the dealer's margin. Condition comes from the dealer, shown as a dealer statement. Every change can be undone.
