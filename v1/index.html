AutoMarketPro Dealer App: listing health

A clickable prototype of the first bet in the case study: fix listings at the moment of listing, then flag the cars that need attention.

  1. Enter the VIN, specs fill themselves
  2. Photo guide: 15 angles, a check shows what's missing
  3. AI description from specs and photos; the dealer edits and confirms
  4. Publish at the quality bar
  5. Weekly: 3 cars need attention, with one-click fixes

Principles: specs only from VIN data, AI writes text but never invents facts, few alerts, each with a reason and a one-click fix, every automatic change can be undone.
